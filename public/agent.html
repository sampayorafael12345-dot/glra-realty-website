Calendar on your phone

This private link is your live calendar feed: follow-ups, client birthdays, closing anniversaries, and your entries. Add it once and your phone stays in sync by itself.

Google Calendar: Settings → Add calendar → From URL → paste the link.
iPhone: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar → paste the link.

Anyone with the link can see the calendar, so keep it to yourself. You can make a fresh link any time. The old one stops working.
