Arthaland · Katipunan

LivForty-six storeys, and a bridge straight into campus.

A vertical neighbourhood at the corner of Katipunan and Esteban Abada.

Liv North is Arthaland's first residential tower outside a central business district, and it is built around one unusual advantage: a private bridgeway that puts residents inside Ateneo Gate 1 on foot. Below that sit the things a student or a young professional actually uses on a weekday, stacked floor by floor rather than spread across a village.

The study nook

A fitted library bench on the amenity level, five minutes from a lecture theatre. Co-working rooms sit alongside it for the days when the unit is not where the work happens.

What makes it work

Four decisions that shape the building.

Three plans, from twenty-four square metres.

Studio 24–29 sqm Built for one, and planned so that every metre does something. The entry unit, and the one that makes the address reachable. 258–312 sqft
One-Bedroom 40–47 sqm A separate bedroom with the living area left open, which is the difference between working from home and living at work. 431–506 sqft
Two-Bedroom 68–70 sqm The family plan, with a balcony off the living area. Also the one buyers take when two students share and split it. 732–753 sqft

Units start at ₱8.3M as published by the developer, subject to change without notice. Current availability, floor plans and a computation against a specific unit are available on request.

Inside.

Katipunan Avenue, Loyola Heights.

Ateneo is across the bridgeway. Miriam and UP Diliman are minutes up the same road, and Katipunan carries the LRT-2 terminus at Katipunan station plus the C5 connection south. The strip itself is the reason people stay in the area after they graduate.
