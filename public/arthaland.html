A developer that builds few things, and certifies all of them.

Arthaland is a Philippine property developer listed on the Philippine Stock Exchange. It puts its buildings through third-party sustainability certification as a matter of course — LEED, WELL, EDGE and BERDE, three international standards and one Philippine, pursued together rather than collected one at a time. GLRA Realty is an accredited seller of the five developments below.

They are not variations on each other. Sondris and Eluria are two very different answers to the same square kilometre of Makati. Liv is a student address on Katipunan and Una is a first home in a Laguna university town. Lucima is finished and standing in Cebu. Each has its own page, built the same way, so you can read them against one another.

What they share

The same four certifications, on every one.

  • LEED
  • WELL
  • EDGE
  • BERDE

Lucima and Eluria are pre-certified LEED Gold. Sondris, Liv and Una are registered and targeting the same four standards. Until a building completes, those are targets rather than awards, and each page says which it is.

Sevina Park, where Una stands, is separately certified Platinum under both LEED for Neighborhood Development and LEED for Homes.

Certification is the part of a developer's claim that somebody else has to sign. It is also the part a buyer can check years later, which is why it is the thread worth following across five buildings that otherwise have very little in common — a 580 sqm penthouse on Rada Street and a 24 sqm studio on Katipunan are not the same product, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
