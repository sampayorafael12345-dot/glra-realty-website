Arthaland · ARCH Capital

EluriaYour neighborhood awaits you.

Thirty-seven residences. Two to a floor.

Eluria is Arthaland's joint venture with ARCH Capital, on 916 square metres at 119 Rada Street. Its architects divided each floor into two elongated homes rather than the usual eight or ten, so every residence draws daylight from three sides and the cross breeze runs the length of it. They describe the result as a contemporary tree house. The name is a portmanteau of élan, flourish and allure.

Legazpi Village

The village

Rada Street closes to cars for the parklets. Greenbelt, Ayala Triangle and the Legazpi Sunday market are all inside a walk, and One Legazpi Park sits across the road.

Design and architecture

Four decisions that shape the building.

A butler, trained in the Netherlands.

Eluria's Hospitality Directors take an intensive programme at The International Butler Academy in the Netherlands before they stand at the door. With thirty-seven households in the building, the service is close to one-to-one, and white-glove in the literal sense.

At the door

  • Concierge
  • Door staff, around the clock
  • Bell and porter service
  • Visitor management
  • Function room service
  • Chauffeured shuttle nearby

For the residence

  • The newspaper, daily
  • Mail and parcel handling
  • Food and drink by cart or tray
  • Escort to your private lift
  • Guests welcomed and announced
  • Amenity reservations

Dining, hotel and venue arrangements, cleaning, laundry, in-residence spa and salon, pet grooming and walking, and a travel desk run through accredited third-party providers rather than the building's own staff.

Four plans. Two of them exist once.

Three-Bedroom North 288 sqm Unit 02 on each residential floor, looking north over Legazpi Village. Plus 10 sqm of limited common area. 3,096 sqft
Three-Bedroom South 294 sqm Unit 01, the south half of the plate, six square metres larger than its neighbour. Plus 10 sqm limited common area. 3,162 sqft
Five-Bedroom Garden 579 sqm A whole floor plate as one home, with its own garden. Plus 21 sqm limited common area. 6,234 sqft
Five-Bedroom Penthouse 580 sqm One only, at the top of the tower under the roof deck garden. Plus 21 sqm limited common area. 6,239 sqft

Every residence is delivered with a gourmet kitchen in European-grade cabinetry, quartz counters, engineered wood floors to the bedrooms, variable refrigerant flow air conditioning and home automation for lighting, cameras and cooling. Pricing, current availability and a computation against a specific unit are available on request.

119 Rada Street, Legazpi Village.

Rada is one of the streets Makati gave back to walking, and the parklets along it are the reason the block reads as residential rather than corporate. The private driveway runs off Rada; the car park entrance is kept around the corner on Rodriguez so the frontage stays quiet.
