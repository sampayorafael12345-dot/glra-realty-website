How to use this: the figures come from the developer's own computation sheet — contract price, discount, reservation fee, down-payment percentage and the number of months to turnover. The charges due at turnover vary a great deal between developers and between local government units, so treat six per cent as a placeholder until you have their schedule. Value-added tax is normally already inside a quoted contract price; a house and lot at ₱3,600,000 or below is VAT-exempt, above it 12% applies.
