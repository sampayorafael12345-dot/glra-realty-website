Arthaland · Mitsui Fudosan

SondrisLetting life in.

Thirty‑seven storeys over the last low‑rise view in Makati.

Sondris is Arthaland's joint venture with Mitsui Fudosan (Asia), the regional arm of one of Japan's largest real estate groups. It stands on Arnaiz Avenue in Legazpi Village, facing the two-storey roofs of San Lorenzo, which is why the views hold. The name compounds sonder, the realisation that every stranger's life is as vivid as your own, with iru, the Japanese for being.
