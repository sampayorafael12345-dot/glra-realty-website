Sondris is Arthaland’s joint venture with Mitsui Fudosan (Asia) — the Japanese developer’s first residential project of this kind in Makati. The name is a compound of sonder, the realisation that every stranger’s life is as vivid as your own, and iru, the Japanese for “being.”

Every residence comes with a choice of three interior packages — Timeless & Transitional, Modern & Industrial, or Formal & Serene — applied across the bedrooms, kitchen, living and dining, and bathrooms. Sustainability & wellness

Why the bills are lower

Arthaland is the only Philippine developer whose entire residential and commercial portfolio is certified sustainable. On Sondris that translates into specific, measurable things rather than a label.

  • Building envelope designed to a 27% glass ratio — daylight without the heat load
  • Double-glazed façade with efficient air-conditioning and lighting, targeting at least 40% off electricity
  • Low-flow plumbing fixtures targeting at least 20% off water
  • Dedicated Outdoor Air System bringing in fresh, filtered air and controlling humidity
  • Laboratory-grade air filters to keep microbes and pathogens out of the unit
  • Carbon monoxide monitors in every residence
  • Airtight units, which stop sound and cooking smells travelling between walls
  • Low-emitting, non-toxic building materials
  • Rainwater collected and stored for irrigation and car washing
  • 67 EV-ready parking slots and 116 bicycle slots with a repair station
  • Bike-friendly elevator and dedicated bike access
  • Endemic and endangered planting to support local biodiversity
