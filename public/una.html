Arthaland · Sevina Park

UnaThree towers in a park, an hour south of Makati.

A first home you can actually move into.

Una Apartments sits inside Sevina Park, Arthaland's estate on Cecilia Araneta Parkway in Biñan, next door to De La Salle University's Laguna campus. Leandro V. Locsin Partners drew it. The units are studios and one-bedrooms with a balcony, a 2.7 metre ceiling and laminated wood floors, and they arrive with the air conditioning, kitchen, bed and wardrobe already in them.

Twenty-eight square metres with a 2.7 metre ceiling, which is what keeps a studio this size from feeling like a corridor. The model unit is styled; the shelving and the objects are not part of it.

What is in it when you get the keys.

Most pre-selling units are handed over bare. These are not. The list below is the developer's own schedule of deliverables, which is worth reading against the photographs: the model units are styled for viewing, and the styling is not what arrives.

Studio · 28 sqm

  • Split-type air conditioning, living and dining
  • Kitchen cabinets and fixtures
  • Dining table for two
  • Double bed frame and mattress
  • Wardrobe cabinet
  • Bathroom mirror

One-Bedroom · 37 sqm

  • Split-type air conditioning, living and dining
  • A second unit in the bedroom
  • Kitchen cabinets and fixtures
  • Two-seater sofa and dining table
  • Queen bed frame and mattress
  • Wardrobe cabinet and bathroom mirror

Arthaland's own note against these photographs: the model units are styled to show design ideas and do not represent the delivered unit. Earlier marketing described the furnishings as supplied by IKEA; the current schedule states that they are no longer sourced from IKEA. Ask for the deliverables sheet for the specific tower before signing anything.

The estate, and the balcony.

Sevina Park, Biñan, Laguna.

Sevina Park is the first masterplanned community in Southeast Asia to hold Platinum certification under both LEED for Neighborhood Development and LEED for Homes. De La Salle University's Laguna campus is next door, and SLEX and CALAX both connect from the parkway, which is what makes the address work for a commute that does not go through Metro Manila traffic.
