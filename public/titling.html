GLRA Land Title Documentation

Title transfer and estate settlement, done for you

We deal with the BIR, the city hall and the Registry of Deeds so you don't have to queue. Tell us your situation and we will tell you exactly what it needs, with a quote before you decide.

What we handle

One team from the first document check to the new title and tax declaration in your name.

Transfer of title

After a sale or a donation: the BIR taxes and eCAR, the transfer tax, registration of the new title, and the new tax declaration.

Estate settlement for heirs

Property still in the name of a parent or grandparent: the settlement document, estate tax, publication, and the transfer to the heirs.

Lost or damaged titles

Reconstitution of an owner's duplicate or an original title that was lost, burned or damaged.

Correction of entry

Misspelled names, wrong civil status, or other errors on the title that block a sale or a loan.

Due diligence before you buy

We verify the title at the Registry of Deeds, check the tax declaration and tax payments, and flag liens or annotations before you pay.

Land titling

For land that has never been titled: we explain the route that fits your property and what it needs.

Inherited a property and want to sell or lease it?

Most inherited properties can't be sold yet: the title is still in the name of the parent who passed away. We settle the estate, put the title in the heirs' names, and, if you want, sell or lease the property through GLRA Realty. One team, one point of contact, from paperwork to buyer.

  1. We check the title, tax declaration and the family documents.
  2. We prepare the settlement and handle the estate tax and publication.
  3. The title is transferred to the heirs.
  4. GLRA Realty markets it to buyers or tenants, if you choose.

What to prepare

The usual starting documents. Every case is a little different, and we will tell you if yours needs more.

After a sale
  • Notarized Deed of Absolute Sale
  • Owner's duplicate copy of the title (TCT or CCT)
  • Latest tax declarations (land and building)
  • Real property tax receipts or tax clearance
  • Valid IDs and TINs of the seller and the buyer
Inherited property
  • Death certificate of the owner
  • Birth certificates of the heirs, and the marriage certificate if married
  • Owner's duplicate copy of the title
  • Tax declarations and real property tax receipts
  • Valid IDs and TINs of all heirs
Owner or heir living abroad
  • A Special Power of Attorney naming the property, signed abroad and apostilled or consularized
  • A copy of the passport or ID of the person signing
  • We can coordinate with your representative here, and update you on Messenger, Viber or email
