Is This Sale VAT-Exempt?

Twelve per cent is a large number on a property. Whether it applies turns on what is being sold and who is selling it, not only on the price — and a bare residential lot lost its own exemption in 2021.

The Sale

Who is selling matters as much as what

This is the first question, not the price. VAT only reaches property sold in the ordinary course of trade or business. A private owner selling a house they lived in is outside VAT entirely.

Two things this page does not decide for you. A seller in business whose gross annual sales stay under ₱3,000,000 may register as non-VAT and pay percentage tax instead, which changes the answer for a small lessor or an occasional dealer. And socialised and economic housing under the housing laws carry their own reliefs. Both are worth an accountant's half hour before you price anything.

Renting rather than selling? The lease side is worked out in full on the rental income tax calculator, which handles the ₱15,000 per-unit line and the 8% option together.

A guide to the rule, not tax advice. Whether a seller is "engaged in business" is a question of fact the BIR decides on the whole picture, not on a dropdown. The threshold is re-indexed to the consumer price index every three years and has moved twice since 2018. Socialised and economic housing, installment sales, and sellers below the ₱3,000,000 registration threshold all have their own treatment. Confirm with your accountant or the BIR before you price, sign or file anything.
