Read the tax declaration, not the price tag: amilyar is charged on the assessed value, which is a percentage of the market value your assessor has on file — usually far below what the property would sell for today. Assessment levels and the basic rate are set by each local government by ordinance and can only go up to the ceilings in the table above, so the figures here start at the legal maximum and are meant to be edited to match your own declaration. Some LGUs give a discount of up to 20% for paying early or in full; many bill quarterly without one. Idle-land tax and special levies are not included.
